# Distributed Martian Atmospheric CO₂ Processing Swarms

**A Conceptual Architecture for Plasma-Vortex ISRU and Autonomous Microreactors**

**Author:** J.P. De Luca  
**Version:** 0.2 — Concept Paper  
**Date:** September 2026

> This repository contains a **speculative, experimental concept study**. It is not a validated mission architecture, demonstrated plasma reactor, terraforming protocol, or engineering specification. The paper explicitly separates demonstrated evidence, literature-supported mechanisms, engineering extrapolation, and long-horizon speculation.

## Concept

The project explores whether **large swarms of autonomous, ultralight atmospheric microdevices** could process Martian CO₂ locally using compact plasma-flow reactors. The preferred Stage-1 chemical target is:

$$
2CO_2 \rightarrow 2CO + O_2
$$

The architecture evolved from free Tesla-coil-like discharge and large fixed infrastructure toward **vortex-stabilized plasma microreactors**, primarily solar-powered and transported or navigated through Martian atmospheric circulation.

A second, deliberately more speculative branch considers **contained biological micro-habitats** as a future experimental extension. It is not proposed as an open biological release under current planetary-protection constraints.

## Central research question

> **Can a compact plasma-flow reactor operating under Mars-relevant pressure achieve enough CO₂ conversion per unit wall-plug energy and unit mass to make distributed atmospheric ISRU competitive with, or complementary to, centralized systems?**

The project deliberately treats this as a **falsifiable engineering question**, not as an established result.

## Evidence status

| Tier | Meaning | Examples in this project |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Demonstrated** | Experimental evidence exists | MOXIE oxygen production on Mars; terrestrial plasma CO₂ dissociation |
| **B — Literature-supported** | Mechanism demonstrated in related configurations | vortex/gliding-arc CO₂ processing; Mars balloon studies |
| **C — Engineering extrapolation** | Proposed architecture requiring validation | flying plasma microreactors; million-unit chemical swarms |
| **D — Highly speculative** | Long-horizon extension | planetary-scale O₂ accumulation; biological micro-habitat deployment |
